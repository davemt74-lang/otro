Stripe

Secrets stay in HomeServer's protected local credential store. VP3 receives normalized checkout, payment, webhook and refund results—not these credentials or card data.

Local HomeServer Stripe currently rejects offers that require a VP3 platform fee. Those offers must use a compatible cloud payment authority until local platform-fee routing is explicitly supported. A payment attempt never silently switches authority after checkout begins.
