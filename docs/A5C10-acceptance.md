## Fixed acceptance failures

- A paused mission with one saved change and an interrupted worker previously reported `complete`. The cross-system lifecycle test reproduced this against A5C9. Outcome reports now show `interrupted`, retain saved-change verification, and state the required owner action.
- Completed research with queued delivery or verification drift was hidden from the collapsed canvas. The real Chromium test failed against the previous script. Both interfaces retain pending delivery and attention states and display the next owner action in the completion report.
- A rejected foreground read previously left command reviews, staffing, schedules and persisted private intents cached. All mission requests now clear those surfaces on authentication, permission or session rejection. Responses begun before revocation cannot restore them. Unrelated preferences are preserved.
- Prepared mission authority is checked before findings and outcome reports are projected.
- A changed verification receipt now resolves its older success notice and produces one current attention notice; repeated readback adds no duplicate.
